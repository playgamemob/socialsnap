Privacy Policy

SocialSnap by PlayGame Team

Effective Date: October 24, 2026
Last Updated: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

PlayGame Team ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the SocialSnap Android application ("App") accesses and uses device permissions and information while providing its features.

SocialSnap allows users to capture photos in dimensions suitable for different social media platforms, save images to their devices, and share photos directly with supported applications. The App also uses Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements and includes a basic VPN-based activity for its ad-blocking functionality.

2. Information We Collect

Camera and Photos

SocialSnap requests camera permission to capture photographs. Images can be adjusted to fit supported social media dimensions and aspect ratios. Photos are saved locally when you choose to save them. We do not intentionally upload captured photos to PlayGame Team's servers.

Storage and Saved Images

The App may request storage or media permissions where required by Android to save images to a separate folder on your device. You can manage or delete saved images using your device's gallery or file manager.

Internet and Advertising Information

The App uses internet access to display advertisements through Google AdMob. Depending on the SDK configuration, Google AdMob may collect or process information such as IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising IDs, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy .

3. VPN-Based Ad-Blocking Activity

SocialSnap includes a basic VPN-based activity intended to support its ad-blocking functionality. This functionality is intended for advertisement filtering and blocking and is not presented as a general-purpose VPN service.

  • The VPN functionality may process network traffic as necessary for its implemented blocking functionality.
  • PlayGame Team does not intentionally collect users' browsing history, passwords, messages, or personal communications through this feature.
  • We do not intentionally store users' browsing activity on our own servers.
  • Any traffic processing, logging, or data transmission depends on the actual implementation of the VPN activity.
  • Android may request user authorization before the VPN functionality can be activated.

The feature does not guarantee that all advertisements will be blocked and is not intended to provide anonymity or comprehensive online privacy protection.

4. How We Use Information

Information and permissions are used to support the following:

  • Capturing photos using the device camera.
  • Adjusting images to supported social media dimensions.
  • Saving photos to a separate folder on the device.
  • Sharing photos through supported applications.
  • Displaying interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Supporting the VPN-based ad-blocking functionality.
  • Maintaining the stability and operation of the App.

We do not sell users' personal information.

5. Sharing of Information

PlayGame Team does not intentionally upload or share users' captured photos through its own servers.

  • Google AdMob: Google and its advertising partners may process device and advertising-related information to deliver advertisements, measure performance, and prevent fraud.
  • Social media applications: When you choose to share a photo, the selected application receives the content you choose to share. Its handling of that content is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements: Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

6. Data Storage and Retention

  • Photos are stored on your device when you choose to save them.
  • We do not intentionally retain copies of your photos on our own servers.
  • Temporary files may be created during photo processing and may be removed by the App or Android.
  • Advertising-related information may be retained by Google or other service providers according to their respective policies.

Photos saved in shared device storage may remain after you uninstall the App. You can delete those photos manually.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. SocialSnap is designed to keep photo capture and saving on your device rather than requiring uploads to our own servers.

However, no software or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security. Users should take care when sharing photos with third-party applications.

8. Permissions Used

  • Camera: To capture photographs.
  • Storage or media access: To save images to a separate folder where required.
  • Internet: To load advertisements and support required online functionality.
  • VPN authorization: To activate the VPN-based ad-blocking activity, if required by the implementation.

Permissions are used for their intended functionality. Denying a permission may prevent the corresponding feature from working.

9. Third-Party Services

SocialSnap integrates Google AdMob for advertising. Google and other integrated third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Help Center
  • Google Terms of Service

10. Children's Privacy

SocialSnap does not intentionally collect personal information from children through its own servers. The App's target audience and advertising configuration must comply with applicable laws and Google Play Families Policy requirements if the App is directed toward children or is otherwise subject to those requirements.

11. Your Choices

You can control your use of SocialSnap by:

  • Granting or revoking camera and storage permissions.
  • Choosing whether to save or share photos.
  • Deleting saved photos through your device.
  • Managing available advertising privacy settings through your device settings.
  • Managing or revoking VPN authorization through Android settings, where supported.
  • Uninstalling the App at any time.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

PlayGame Team may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, permissions, advertising services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at the public URL where this policy is hosted. The last updated date will be revised when appropriate.
